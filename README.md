Update because i found error to raw links and i have solve:
Unfortunately, for some reason I can't fathom, raw links refuse to import. However, here is a workaround: go to the specific file, copy its entire contents, open Happ, tap the "+" button, and paste from your clipboard. Happ should automatically adapt to your device's language, but if it doesn't, look for the option labeled "Paste from clipboard"—or simply use the second button within the "+" menu. I will be uploading configurations periodically; they might not work for everyone due to various issues, but I will do my best to find the best ones for you, my dear European friends.
